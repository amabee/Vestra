# Browse your sources

Choose a streaming extension in Anime. Its selection stays in the URL and follows
you into details and playback. Back and Forward restore the selected source.

## Home and saved lists

Home shows Continue Watching from real incomplete progress, Currently Watching,
Plan to Watch, Completed, On Hold and Dropped, plus named playlists. Status controls
update the existing saved list. Playback also saves history snapshots so an anime
can appear in Continue Watching without being bookmarked first.

## Discover anime

Discover offers a featured title and horizontally scrollable Trending, Top Anime
of the Season, Coming Soon and Top Anime Movies shelves. Use dropdowns for genre,
release year, release status, season and format. Filters remain in the URL.
A releasing icon marks currently airing titles. Episode counts are broadcast
counts from AniList, not a claim that an extension can stream every episode.
Unknown counts remain unannounced.

## Follow a weekly schedule

Schedule uses titles from saved lists, playlists and watching history. Choose the
week, **Week begins**, and list status. Times are displayed in your local timezone.
The Show Watched Episodes switch hides only confirmed watched episodes. Older
progress without an episode number remains visible rather than guessing from an
opaque identifier. Playback and manual episode marking now retain known numbers.
Titles from source-specific lists need an exact catalogue match; unresolved titles
are listed separately. Historical schedules may be incomplete and broadcasts can
differ from extension upload times.

## Open Browse sources

The Browse sources tab uses your selected installed extension. Choose a discovery
category and turn pages without retyping a title. Suggested, ongoing, latest,
upcoming and popular are the standard category names.

Native extensions can supply feeds through the optional `browse` method. Only the
categories declared by that extension are offered as source feeds. Results retain
the source's own IDs and open its episodes directly.

## Sources without discovery feeds

Older sources may support only search, episodes and streams. For those, Vestra offers
labelled AniList catalogue discovery with your chosen playback source retained.
Catalogue discovery does not establish source availability. Episodes are matched
when you open a title; upcoming titles may not have episodes. Use direct source
search when a title has a different name or edition.

## Search and save

Search in the Anime workspace to find catalogue titles and source matches. Play
results, add them to Watch later or save them to named playlists. Saved source
matches retain stable title identities instead of temporary handles.

## Missing or disabled sources

An unavailable selected source is reported instead of silently replaced. Open
Extensions to enable it, or choose another source. Late results from a replaced
source cannot populate the new selection.
